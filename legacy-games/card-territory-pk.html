How to Play

Texas Hold'em — best 5-card hand wins the pot

Goal

Make the best 5-card hand from your 2 hole cards plus 5 shared community cards.

Betting Rounds

Pre-flop (after hole cards) → Flop (3 community cards) → Turn (1 more) → River (1 more) → Showdown.

Actions

Fold — give up the hand. Check/Call — match the current bet or pass if no bet is on. Raise — increase the bet. All-In — bet everything you have.
