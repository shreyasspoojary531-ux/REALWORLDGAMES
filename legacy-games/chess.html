How to Play

Quick reference for chess & app controls

Goal

Checkmate the opposing king — trap it under attack with no safe square, block, or capture to escape.

Moves

Tap a piece, then tap a highlighted square to move there. Rotate your phone sideways for a landscape layout — the board and controls reflow automatically.

Special moves

Castling, en passant, and pawn promotion are all supported — the app handles the rules for you automatically.

Help

Playing vs Computer gives you unlimited move highlights and hints — turn them off anytime with the 💡 badge if you want a real challenge. Two Players and Online Multiplayer have no help at all: no highlights, no hints, just the rules.

Controls

↺ undoes the last move. 💡 asks the engine for a hint (vs Computer only). ⇅ flips the board.

Levels

Twenty bot strengths from Beginner to World Champion. Win at your current top level to unlock the next.
