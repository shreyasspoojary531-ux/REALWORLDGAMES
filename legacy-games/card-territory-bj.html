How to Play

Blackjack — beat the dealer without going over 21

Goal

Get a hand value closer to 21 than the dealer, without exceeding it.

Card Values

Number cards = face value. Face cards (J, Q, K) = 10. Ace = 1 or 11, whichever helps your hand more.

Turn Actions

Hit — take another card. Stand — keep your current total. Double — double your bet, take exactly one more card. Split — if your first two cards match, split into two hands.

Dealer Rules

Dealer reveals their hidden card once you finish. Dealer must hit until reaching 17 or more, then stands.

Payouts

Win: 1:1. Blackjack (Ace + 10-value in first two cards): 3:2. Push (tie): bet returned. Bust: bet lost.
