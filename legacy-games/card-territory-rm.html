How to Play

13-Card Rummy — form sets and sequences

Goal

Arrange all 13 cards into valid groups, then declare before your opponent.

Groups

Sequence — 3+ consecutive cards, same suit. Set — 3 or 4 cards of the same rank, different suits.

To Declare

You need at least two sequences, and every one of your 13 cards must belong to a valid group.

Turn

Draw one card from the stock pile or the discard pile, then discard one card from your hand. Tap "Sort Hand" to group by suit for easier planning.
