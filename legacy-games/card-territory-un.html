How to Play

Uno — match color or number, empty your hand first

Goal

Be the first to play all your cards.

Playing a Card

Match the top discard by color or number (or symbol). Wild cards can be played anytime and let you choose the color.

Action Cards

Skip — next player loses their turn. Reverse — play direction flips. +2 — next player draws 2 and is skipped. Wild +4 — choose a color, next player draws 4 and is skipped.

No Playable Card?

Draw one card from the pile. If it's playable, you may play it immediately.

Call UNO!

When you're down to your last card, tap "Call UNO!" before your turn ends — or risk a penalty.
