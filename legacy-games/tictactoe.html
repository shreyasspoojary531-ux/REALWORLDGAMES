RULES

Objective

Be the first to line up your marks in a row — a row, column, or diagonal — on the grid. On the classic 3×3 board that's 3 in a row; bigger boards need longer runs (up to 5 in a row on 7×7).

Turns

Players alternate placing one mark per turn on any empty cell. Whoever completes the required run first wins that game. If the whole grid fills with no line, it's a draw.

Boards Grow With You

Campaign starts on a classic 3×3 grid and grows — 4×4, 5×5, 6×6, all the way to 7×7 — as you clear tiers, each one needing a longer run to win. Quick Match and Local 1v1 let you pick any board size directly.

Campaign Series

Most Campaign levels are played as a short series of games, not just one. Win enough games (or, against the toughest bots, avoid losing any) to clear the level and unlock the next.

The Late Tiers

Tic-tac-toe is a solved game on a 3×3 board — perfect play from both sides always ends in a draw. Once the bot plays flawlessly, the goal quietly shifts from "win" to "never lose."

Grandmaster & Beyond

Flawless bot, a bigger board, and a countdown per move. Miss the clock and a random cell gets played for you.
