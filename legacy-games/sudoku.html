RULES

The goal

Fill the 9×9 grid so every row, every column, and every bold 3×3 box contains the digits 1 through 9 exactly once. No repeats, anywhere, ever.

Making a move

Tap an empty cell, then tap a number on the pad below. Cells with bold numbers are givens and can't be changed. Tap ✏️ Notes to switch into pencil-mark mode and jot down candidates in the corners of a cell instead of committing to an answer.

Tools

Erase clears the selected cell. Undo steps back your last move. Hint reveals the correct digit for a cell for a small coin cost — useful, but it trims your reward at the end.

Auto-Check

When on (default), a wrong digit is flagged red immediately and counts as a mistake. Turn it off in Settings for a stricter, old-school challenge where nothing is confirmed until the board is full.

Versus modes

In Practice vs Bot, Local 1v1/2v2 and Wager Match, players share one puzzle and take turns. A correct entry scores a point and keeps your turn — a wrong one is a foul and passes to the next player. Whoever completes the most cells correctly (or finishes the grid first) wins.

Levels

The Level campaign is a single, ongoing ladder — puzzles start easy and quietly get harder the further you go. There's no final level; it's built to keep going.

AUTO-CHECK

Auto-Check instantly flags a wrong number in red the moment you place it, and it's what lets the turn-based versus modes work fairly (a wrong guess has to be visible right away so the turn can pass).

It's on by default in single-player modes too. If you'd rather solve without the safety net — nothing confirmed until the grid is full — flip it off here or in Settings. In versus modes it always stays on so turns can be scored.
