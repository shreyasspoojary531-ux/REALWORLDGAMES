RULES

Objective

Pocket all 9 of your team's coins (White or Black) plus the Queen before your opponent.

Turns

Drag back from the striker and release to shoot. Pocketing your own coin earns a point and an extra turn. Pocketing your opponent's coin gives the point to them instead, and passes the turn.

The Queen

Pocketing the gold Queen scores nothing immediately — you get one grace shot to cover it by pocketing one of your own coins. Succeed and you bank +5 and keep shooting. Fail, and the Queen returns to the center, uncovered.

Fouls

Pocketing the striker itself is a foul: -1 point, and the turn passes immediately. If the Queen was pending cover when you foul, it also returns to the table.

Winning

Once every coin is off the table, the side with the higher score wins.

HELP & AIM TRAINER

Aim Trainer

While pulling back the striker in Practice vs Bot, a dotted line shows exactly where the striker will travel — including wall bounces — before you let go.

Turning it off

It's on by default in every mode. If you'd rather play without the assist, flip it off here or in Settings — it applies everywhere immediately.

Lining up a shot

Two separate controls: slide the POSITION rail below the board to line the striker up along your edge, then drag back directly on the striker itself and release to fire — touching the striker never fires by itself, only a real pull-back does.

Power

The pull-back distance sets your power — short pull, soft tap; long pull, full-power strike. Watch the meter at the bottom of the board.
